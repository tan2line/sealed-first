Privacy

Sealed First is built so your seals stay with you.

The browser app

Everything you enter in the app at sealedfirst.com/app is saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to any server. Clearing your browser data deletes it. Export your log to keep a copy.

The app sends nothing to an AI. You copy the question into the AI of your choice and paste its answer back.

The skills

The skills save your log to a file on your own computer. The only thing they send to an AI model is the question you ask the blind agent, and that goes through the AI tool you already use, under its own terms.

This website

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. There are no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics scripts.

Team pilots

Pilots record case reference numbers and reviewer decisions, never names or personal details about a case. Each pilot's terms are agreed in writing before it starts.
